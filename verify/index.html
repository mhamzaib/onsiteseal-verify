Use a single-photo zip or a whole-job zip (all photos checked). Or pick one JPEG and its .receipt.json. Nothing is sent to OnSiteSeal.

or

What a pass proves: the JPEG bytes match the receipt hash (SHA-256), and the receipt claims were signed with the public key embedded in that receipt (Ed25519). Whole-job zips are checked photo-by-photo — overall pass means every pair passed.

What it does not prove: that the key came from a genuine OnSiteSeal install or hardware attestation (a replaced photo+receipt pair signed with any key can still “pass”), what the camera was pointed at, unspoofable GPS, correct clock, who held the phone, or court admissibility.

Signing mode software means device-protected key storage with software Ed25519 — useful portable integrity, not ATTESTED hardware.
