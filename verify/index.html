What a pass proves: the JPEG bytes match the receipt hash (SHA-256), and the receipt claims were signed with the device key embedded in the receipt (Ed25519).

What it does not prove: what the camera was pointed at, that GPS could not be spoofed on a compromised device, that the clock was correct, who held the phone, hardware attestation, or that evidence is automatically court-admissible.

Signing mode software means device-protected key storage with software Ed25519 — useful portable integrity, not ATTESTED hardware.
