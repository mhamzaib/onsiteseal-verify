Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Sealed on site. Verifiable anywhere. Jobs and photos stay on your device unless you share them. There is no OnSiteSeal account and no OnSiteSeal cloud sync in this MVP.

Summary

OnSiteSeal is a local-first proof-of-work camera. Capture, watermark, sealed receipts, PDFs, and backups live in the app on your phone. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the current app. Reverse geocoding may send coordinates to platform services or OpenStreetMap Nominatim when you are online.

Data on your device

The app stores job metadata, photos, GPS claims, optional photo labels/notes, proof receipts, and reports in on-device storage (SQLite + app documents). An Ed25519 signing seed is kept in platform SecureStore for sealing receipts.

Permissions: camera; precise and coarse location while using the app. We do not request background location when the app is killed.

Data transmitted off your device

Reverse geocoding

When GPS quality is good and you are online, the app may reverse-geocode latitude and longitude to a short address stamp:

  • Platform / native geocoder first (via the OS). On many Android devices this uses Google or OEM location services, which may receive your coordinates under their policies.
  • Fallback: OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) over HTTPS, sending lat/lon and a product User-Agent. OSM/Nominatim operators may log requests per their usage policies.

Weak or unavailable GPS uses honest on-device stamps without inventing a street address from a coarse pin.

Sharing you start

Share proof packs, job packs, PDFs, or backups through the OS share sheet. Files go to the destination you choose — not to an OnSiteSeal cloud.

Browser verifier

The static verifier checks hashes and signatures in your browser. Proof files are not uploaded to OnSiteSeal servers.

What we do not collect (MVP)

  • No OnSiteSeal accounts or cloud job sync
  • No first-party analytics SDK
  • No crash-reporting SDK
  • No advertising ID collection by OnSiteSeal

Purpose / sharing / retention

We use on-device data to provide capture, geotag stamps, sealing, reports, and backup/restore. We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from app content.

Recipient What When
Platform geocoder Lat/lon Native reverse geocode
OSM Nominatim Lat/lon (HTTPS) Fallback reverse geocode
Your share destinations Files you export Only when you share

Retention on device lasts until you delete content, clear app storage, uninstall, or restore a replace backup. Backup zips are unencrypted user exports; signing keys in SecureStore are not included in backups. Third-party geocoders follow their own retention rules. OnSiteSeal does not store your job library on our servers in this MVP.

Security (honest scope)

  • App sandbox storage; OS disk encryption when the device enables it.
  • Signing seed in SecureStore; seal signatures are software Ed25519 — not hardware-attested Keystore/SE signing.
  • Nominatim calls use HTTPS.
  • GPS spoofing on compromised devices cannot be ruled out absolutely.

Children’s privacy

OnSiteSeal is for contractors and trades, not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. Contact us via the placeholder above for help removing on-device data (uninstall / clear storage).

Your choices

  • Deny camera or location permissions (features will be limited).
  • Delete jobs/photos in the app, or uninstall to remove local data.
  • Stay offline to avoid reverse-geocode network calls (address may show coordinates).
  • Share packs only with people and services you trust.
